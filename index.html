Division 01

Coffee Farming & Harvesting

Premium coffee grown under optimal highland conditions and harvested with precision to preserve bean integrity and flavor potential.

Our coffee estates combine traditional knowledge with modern agronomy. Shade-grown plots, selective hand-picking at peak ripeness, and controlled wet or dry processing deliver consistent specialty-grade lots.

  • Elevation-optimized arabica & robusta cultivars
  • Selective hand-harvesting of ripe cherries only
  • On-site wet mill and drying facilities
  • Full traceability from plot to export lot
  • Soil health and biodiversity management
☕ Coffee Estates
Division 02

Pig Farming

High-welfare pig production systems engineered for animal health, efficient growth, and reliable pork supply.

We operate modern breeding and finishing units with strict biosecurity, balanced nutrition, and continuous health monitoring. Our focus is on producing quality pork while maintaining high standards of animal care and environmental responsibility.

  • Closed-herd breeding with performance genetics
  • Climate-controlled housing & enrichment
  • Precision feeding and growth tracking
  • Veterinary protocols & vaccination schedules
  • Manure management & nutrient recycling
🐷 Pig Production
Division 03

Fishery Farming

Responsible aquaculture delivering fresh, high-quality fish through managed ponds and efficient water systems.

Our fishery operations prioritize water quality, sustainable stocking densities, and careful species selection. From hatchery to harvest, every stage is monitored to ensure healthy fish and minimal environmental impact.

  • Freshwater species suited to local conditions
  • Pond and recirculating aquaculture systems
  • Continuous water-quality monitoring
  • Feed optimization & disease prevention
  • Responsible harvest & cold-chain handling
AQUACULTURE Healthy waters. Sustainable growth.
Division 04

Chicken Farming

Integrated poultry systems producing quality broiler meat and table eggs with strong biosecurity and welfare standards.

Our chicken operations cover both meat and egg production. Birds are raised in well-ventilated housing with controlled lighting, nutrition programs, and rigorous health management to ensure consistent, safe products.

  • Broiler and commercial layer flocks
  • Climate-controlled housing & ventilation
  • Formulated feeds for growth & laying
  • Strict biosecurity & vaccination programs
  • Hygienic processing & egg collection
