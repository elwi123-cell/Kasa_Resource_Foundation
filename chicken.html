Division 04 · Poultry

From Placement to Product

Our chicken operations cover both meat and egg production. Birds are raised in well-ventilated, climate-controlled housing with stage-specific nutrition and rigorous health management to ensure consistent, safe products.

Broiler flocks are managed to target live-weight and uniformity specifications for processors. Layer flocks are managed for egg quality, shell strength, and production persistence. Collection, grading, and packing systems maintain hygiene from farm to customer.

  • Climate-controlled housing with ventilation management
  • Stage-specific nutrition for growth and laying performance
  • Strict biosecurity and vaccination programmes
  • Traceable flocks from placement to dispatch
  • Hygienic egg collection, grading, and packing

🐔 Poultry Systems
Housing & Health

Environment, Nutrition & Biosecurity

Housing is designed for effective ventilation, temperature control, and stocking densities that support welfare and performance. Lighting programmes and feed formulations are stage-specific.

Vaccination schedules and hygiene protocols protect flocks from common poultry diseases. Restricted access, disinfection routines, and controlled movement of staff and equipment limit the introduction and spread of pathogens.

Climate Control

Ventilation, temperature, and humidity managed to reduce stress and support feed conversion and laying rates.

Nutrition

Formulated feeds matched to growth phase or laying stage for efficiency and product quality.

Biosecurity

Restricted access, disinfection points, and vaccination programmes to safeguard flock health.

Welfare

Stocking densities, enrichment, and monitoring aligned with responsible production standards.

Production Cycle

From Placement to Dispatch

A structured pathway for both broiler and layer flocks with full flock records and product integrity.

1

Placement

Day-old chicks or pullets placed into prepared housing. Environmental conditions stabilised before arrival.

2

Grow / Lay

Stage-specific feed, lighting, and health protocols. Continuous monitoring of weight, feed intake, and egg output.

3

Health Checks

Scheduled vaccination, observation, and veterinary support. Early response to any health concerns.

4

Harvest / Collection

Broilers dispatched to target weight; eggs collected, graded, and packed under hygienic conditions.

Quality Assurance

Product Quality & Flock Traceability

Every flock is supported by placement-to-dispatch records. Buyers can link poultry meat or eggs back to housing unit, feed regime, and health history.

Broiler batches are managed for live-weight uniformity and processor specifications. Eggs are graded for size, shell quality, and cleanliness. Hygienic collection and packing maintain product integrity from farm to customer.

  • Flock records from placement through dispatch
  • Weight and uniformity monitoring for broilers
  • Egg grading for size, shell, and cleanliness
  • Hygienic collection and packing systems
  • Documentation aligned with food-safety expectations
