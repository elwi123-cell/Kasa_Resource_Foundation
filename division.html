☕ Coffee Estates
Division 01 Specialty Crop

Coffee Farming & Harvesting

Highland coffee production focused on cup quality, selective harvesting, and controlled post-harvest processing for consistent specialty-grade lots.

Our estates are situated at elevations that favour slow cherry maturation and complex flavour development. We cultivate both arabica and robusta varieties under shade or semi-shade systems that support soil health and biodiversity. Harvest is performed by hand at peak ripeness only; overripe or underripe fruit is excluded from premium lots.

On-site wet mills and raised drying beds allow us to manage fermentation, washing, and drying with precision. Every lot is tagged from plot through parchment and green bean stages, enabling full traceability for buyers and exporters.

Cultivars & Elevation

Arabica and robusta selections matched to altitude bands for optimal cup profile and yield stability.

Harvest Method

Selective hand-picking of ripe cherries; multiple passes per season to maintain quality consistency.

Processing

Wet and dry processing options with controlled fermentation, parchment drying, and moisture monitoring.

Traceability

Plot-level records linked to export lots; cupping notes and physical quality data retained for each shipment.

  • Shade-grown and agroforestry-integrated plantings
  • Soil testing and organic matter management programs
  • Pest and disease monitoring with integrated controls
  • On-site wet mill capacity and raised-bed drying infrastructure
  • Quality grading and cupping before lot release

🐷 Pig Production
Division 02 Livestock

Pig Farming

Modern, welfare-oriented pig production covering breeding, farrowing, weaning, and finishing under strict biosecurity and nutrition protocols.

We operate closed-herd systems with performance-oriented genetics. Housing is climate-controlled with enrichment features to support natural behaviour. Feed formulations are adjusted by growth stage to optimise feed conversion while meeting nutritional requirements for health and carcass quality.

Veterinary oversight includes scheduled vaccination, health monitoring, and rapid response protocols. Manure is managed as a nutrient resource and integrated into our broader farm nutrient cycles where practical, reducing external fertiliser demand.

Breeding & Genetics

Closed-herd breeding with selection for growth rate, feed efficiency, and maternal traits.

Housing Systems

Climate-controlled units with ventilation, enrichment, and space allowances aligned to welfare standards.

Nutrition

Stage-specific rations; continuous monitoring of intake and growth performance.

Health & Biosecurity

Controlled access, vaccination schedules, and veterinary protocols to minimise disease risk.

  • Performance recording from farrowing through finish
  • Strict entry and hygiene protocols for staff and visitors
  • Manure handling designed for nutrient recovery
  • Regular welfare assessments and environmental enrichment
  • Traceable batches from birth to dispatch

🐟 Aquaculture
Division 03 Aquaculture

Fishery Farming

Sustainable freshwater aquaculture producing high-quality fish through managed ponds and, where appropriate, recirculating systems with continuous water-quality oversight.

Species selection is matched to local climate and market demand. Stocking densities are managed to maintain water quality and fish health. Feed is formulated for efficient conversion and reduced environmental loading. Regular sampling of dissolved oxygen, pH, temperature, and ammonia guides daily management decisions.

Harvest is planned to deliver fish at optimal size and condition. Cold-chain handling from pond to packing preserves freshness and supports food-safety standards for buyers in retail and food-service channels.

Production Systems

Earthen ponds and selected recirculating aquaculture units suited to species and scale.

Water Management

Continuous monitoring of key parameters; aeration and exchange protocols to protect stock health.

Feeding & Growth

Formulated feeds, feeding schedules, and growth tracking to optimise conversion and size uniformity.

Harvest & Handling

Planned harvest windows, careful crowding and transport, and rapid chilling for quality retention.

  • Species adapted to regional conditions and market preferences
  • Disease prevention through water quality and biosecurity
  • Feed optimisation to limit nutrient discharge
  • Record-keeping from stocking to harvest lot
  • Support for cold-chain logistics to market

🐔 Poultry Systems
Division 04 Poultry

Chicken Farming

Integrated broiler and layer operations focused on bird health, feed efficiency, biosecurity, and consistent supply of poultry meat and table eggs.

Housing is designed for effective ventilation, temperature control, and stocking densities that support welfare and performance. Lighting programs and feed formulations are stage-specific. Vaccination schedules and hygiene protocols protect flocks from common poultry diseases.

Broiler flocks are managed to target live-weight and uniformity specifications for processors. Layer flocks are managed for egg quality, shell strength, and production persistence. Collection, grading, and packing systems maintain hygiene and product integrity from farm to customer.

Broiler Production

Controlled-environment housing, precision feeding, and growth monitoring to meet processor specifications.

Layer Production

Commercial layers managed for egg quality, consistent output, and shell integrity.

Biosecurity

Restricted access, disinfection routines, and vaccination programs to safeguard flock health.

Product Handling

Hygienic collection, grading, and packing for eggs; coordinated dispatch for live birds or processed product.

  • Climate-controlled housing with ventilation management
  • Stage-specific nutrition for growth and laying performance
  • Regular health monitoring and veterinary support
  • Traceable flocks from placement to dispatch
  • Standards aligned with food-safety expectations of buyers
