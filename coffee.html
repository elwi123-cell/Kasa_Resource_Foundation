From Highlands to Export Lot

Our coffee estates are positioned at elevations that support slow cherry maturation and complex flavour development. We combine traditional selective harvesting with modern agronomy and on-site processing control.

Both arabica and robusta cultivars are matched to altitude bands and microclimates. Shade and semi-shade systems protect soil structure, support biodiversity, and moderate temperature extremes. Every premium lot is built from cherries picked only at peak ripeness.

  • Elevation-optimised cultivar placement
  • Shade-grown and agroforestry-integrated plantings
  • Selective hand-picking with multiple harvest passes
  • On-site wet mill and raised-bed drying infrastructure
  • Plot-to-lot traceability with cupping records

Agronomy & Field Operations

Cultivation & Selective Harvesting

Healthy trees and careful picking are the foundation of cup quality. We manage soil health, canopy, and pest pressure so that cherries reach full maturity under stable conditions.

Harvest teams make multiple passes through each block, collecting only ripe fruit. Underripe and overripe cherries are excluded from specialty lots. This discipline preserves flavour potential and reduces defects downstream.

Soil & Nutrition

Regular soil testing, organic matter programmes, and balanced nutrient applications tailored to elevation and cultivar.

Canopy Management

Pruning and shade regulation to maintain light penetration, airflow, and sustainable yields.

Pest & Disease

Integrated monitoring and controls prioritising prevention and minimal environmental impact.

Harvest Discipline

Hand selection of ripe cherries only; multiple passes per season for consistent maturity.

Post-Harvest

Processing Pathway

Controlled steps from cherry to green bean protect quality and enable consistent, traceable lots for buyers.

1

Reception & Sorting

Cherries received, weighed, and sorted. Defective or mixed-maturity fruit is separated before processing.

2

Pulping & Fermentation

Wet processing with controlled fermentation times. Monitoring ensures clean mucilage removal without over-fermentation.

3

Washing & Drying

Thorough washing followed by raised-bed or patio drying. Moisture is tracked to target export levels.

4

Milling & Grading

Parchment hulling, density and size grading, and final quality checks before lot formation and storage.

Quality Assurance

Cup Quality & Lot Traceability

Every export lot is supported by physical and sensory data. Buyers can link green bean quality back to estate, elevation band, and process method.

Cupping is performed on representative samples before release. Moisture, density, and defect scores are recorded. Lot codes connect field blocks, process batches, and shipment documentation for full supply-chain visibility.

  • Plot-level harvest and process records
  • Moisture and physical quality monitoring
  • Pre-shipment cupping and sensory notes
  • Lot coding from estate through export
  • Documentation ready for commercial and specialty buyers
